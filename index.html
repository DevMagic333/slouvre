protocol

how every launch works from here on. if i break one of these, screenshot it and post it.

"launch" means the moment the contract is revealed to the room and anyone can buy it. creating the token quietly, hours or days earlier, is not a launch — nobody knows it exists and nobody can be sniped.

  1. fresh wallet per launch. funded from an exchange, never from a wallet on this page.
  2. no bundles, no volume bots, no fake buyers.
  3. silent create. no countdown, no announcement.
  4. the contract goes to the telegram room first. x gets it hours or days later.
  5. wallet ownership is proven after launch, never before.
  6. any creator allocation is one disclosed wallet, small, and named in the same message that reveals the contract — so nobody can buy without knowing. never multiple wallets. it gets locked and the lock is shown.
  7. the flat chart gets posted after every launch as the receipt.
  8. one name, one token, ever. a resurrection is declared here and named as such. the old contract is marked retired.
  9. all wallets published — prize, index, tests, duplicates. never "i don't have one." always "here's where it is."
no creator allocation on any launch. creator fees stay on — that's what pays for the sites, the domains, and the prizes. every wallet that collects them is published on this page.
standing rule: nothing launches until the room has a pulse. that means about a hundred real people in the telegram, not a hundred wallets.
slouvre launched at six people in the room. the hundred-person rule was written for launches where the room is the only distribution — slouvre ships with a free site that works without the coin, so it isn't. i'm noting it here instead of quietly editing the rule.
