Six poses · 512×512 · transparent · identical scale across the set

For Telegram: message @Stickers, send /newpack, name the pack, then send the six files as files — not as photos. Sending them as photos strips the transparency and flattens them onto white. Give each one its label as the emoji-free keyword when prompted.

WebP is what Telegram uses internally and runs far smaller than PNG. SVG is the master — it stays sharp at any size, so keep those for print, the site, or anything larger later.
